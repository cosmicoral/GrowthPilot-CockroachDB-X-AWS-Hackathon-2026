# Campaign Analysis

Analyze campaign performance data and identify useful growth insights.

## Instructions

1. Identify the most important performance metrics.
2. Compare actual performance against available benchmarks or expectations.
3. Identify significant strengths, weaknesses, and trends.
4. Avoid inventing metrics or conclusions that are not supported by the input.
5. Produce concise, actionable recommendations.
6. Clearly distinguish observed facts from inferred conclusions.
